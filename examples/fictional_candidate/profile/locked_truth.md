# Locked Truth: Sam Okafor (fictional example)

*Everything in this example is invented, including the person, the employers and every figure. It shows what a filled-in `locked_truth.md` looks like.*

## Figures, locked

- Network: three distribution centers, ~2.4M orders a year (current). Not 2.5M, not "millions."
- Operating budget: $38M annual, with budget accountability. Not P&L: Sam does not own revenue.
- Team: 9 direct managers, ~450 frontline staff across sites. Peak 610 during holiday season: interview material, never resume headcount.
- Network grew from one site to three over six years, across two roles. Never attribute it to the Director role alone.

## Metric bank, verified

- Order cycle time: 6 days (2022) to 2 days (2024), held since. Speed.
- Carrier cost cut 9% through a 2023 rebid. Cost.
- Returns resale channel: $1.4M a year (2024 and 2025), on a grading standard now used at every site. Revenue.
- Pick accuracy: 99.1% to 99.7% (2023 to 2025). Quality.
- Promotions: five frontline leads promoted to site manager since 2021. Talent.
- Slotting tool: 30 staff hours saved in one month during a single re-slot project. One-time; never annualize.

### Prior roles (Fernhill Supply Co.)

- Second distribution center opened in three months, on budget (2019). Network one site to two.
- Vendor compliance scorecard adopted for every inbound supplier. Non-numeric proof (Q2: adopted as standard).
- Dock-to-stock time: 48 hours to 20 (2019 to 2020).
- First network peak plan: on-time shipping held at 98% through the 2019 holiday peak.
- Second shift: 300,000-square-foot facility, 120 staff (scope).
- OSHA recordable incidents down 40% over two years (2015 to 2017).
- Temporary labor spend down 12% after the labor model rebuild (2016).
- Throughput reporting used for staffing decisions for three years (2012 to 2015).

## Figures to verify before use

- Overtime reduction from cross-training: Sam believes ~15%, no report found. Not usable yet.

## Framing rules

- Why moving: the next level, a multi-region network role, does not exist at Kestrel's size. Never dissatisfaction.
- "Budget accountability," never "P&L ownership."
- The marketplace fulfillment pilot was handed to the e-commerce team in 2025: past tense everywhere.
- Kestrel's top retail partners stay unnamed.
- The automation pilot (goods-to-person robotics at one site) is in progress: purpose only, present tense, no results.

## Never claim

- P&L ownership. A team of 610. Annualized slotting-tool savings. The 15% overtime figure.

## Title treatment

- Headlines: see `tracks.md`.
- Current role line: Director, Fulfillment Operations | Kestrel Home Goods | Remote
- Prior titles, official: Senior Manager, Distribution Operations; Operations Manager; Operations Analyst.
- Employer names: Kestrel Home Goods; Fernhill Supply Co.

## Skills

- Defensible: warehouse management systems (Manhattan, Blue Yonder), labor planning, slotting, carrier contracting, lean and 5S, SQL (read and interpret), Power BI, safety programs (OSHA recordables)
- Out: Python, robotics engineering
- Real but not recent: cold-chain operations (2016 to 2018)

## Hard gaps

- No international logistics. No people leadership above ~450. No engineering ownership of automation.

## Interview material

- Holiday peak with 610 staff. The cross-training program. The 2021 site launch under a three-month deadline.

## Voice

- Uses: "throughput," "the floor," "held it."
- Never: "synergy," "spearheaded," "passionate."
