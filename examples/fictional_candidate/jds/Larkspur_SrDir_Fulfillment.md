# Larkspur Outdoor (fictional): Senior Director, Fulfillment Operations

**Source:** fictional posting written for the toolkit example. Found via a board feed, 2026-01-05.
**Location:** Remote, with monthly travel to sites. **Reports to:** VP, Supply Chain.

## About the role
Larkspur is scaling from two fulfillment centers to four over the next three years. The Senior Director owns fulfillment operations across the network and leads the site leadership team through that growth, while holding cost per order and service levels through peak.

## Responsibilities
- Lead fulfillment operations across a multi-node network, with accountability for the operating budget.
- Build the site launch playbook and lead two new site openings.
- Drive cost per order down while holding on-time shipping through peak.
- Partner with engineering on a warehouse automation roadmap.
- Develop site leaders and build a bench for new sites.
- Own carrier strategy and parcel cost.

## Qualifications
- 12+ years in distribution or fulfillment operations, 5+ leading multi-site teams.
- Experience opening new fulfillment sites.
- Track record reducing cost per order at scale.
- Experience with warehouse automation programs.
- WMS experience (Manhattan preferred).
- International logistics experience a plus.
