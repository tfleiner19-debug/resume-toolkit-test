# Locked Truth (source of record)

Every number, title and claim that may appear on a resume, LinkedIn profile or cover letter comes from this file. Anything not here gets flagged for verification, never invented or rounded. Where another file disagrees with this one, this one governs.

**How to fill it.** Replace each `[open]` with your facts. Keep the example lines' *shape*, then delete the examples. Lines in *italics* are guidance. A worked version on a fictional person is in `examples/fictional_candidate/profile/locked_truth.md`.

---

## Figures, locked

*One line per figure. State the figure, its status (active, past, estimate) and the forms it must never take. The "not X, not Y" clause matters: the most damaging resume errors are figures that drifted between versions.*

- [open]
- *Example: Program A: $2.1M annual (active, renewed for next year). Not $2M, not $2.5M.*
- *Example: Team: 12 standing. Peak 16 during a merger. The 16 is interview material, never resume headcount.*
- *Example: Growth from $3M to $9M over five years across two roles. Never attribute it to the current role alone.*

## Framing rules

*How specific facts must be described. These are the rules that took the longest to settle in the original project, because each one stopped a real overclaim.*

- Why you're moving: [open] *(state it as a structural reason, never dissatisfaction. Example: "the next level of this function does not exist at a company my employer's size.")*
- Scope words: [open] *(Example: "budget accountability," never "P&L ownership," unless you hold a P&L.)*
- Past vs. present: [open] *(Example: "a program I built moved to another team last year: past tense everywhere.")*
- Reach vs. use: [open] *(Example: "made available to 5,000 people" is reach. Never write it as adoption or usage without usage data.)*
- Confidentiality: [open] *(Example: clients stay unnamed on resumes and LinkedIn; employer revenue is never stated.)*
- In-progress work: states purpose only, present tense, no results, until it ships.
